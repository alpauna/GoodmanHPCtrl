Advanced Pin Info

GPIO45/46 double-booked: both are ESP32-S3 hardware strapping pins (VDD_SPI voltage select and boot-mode/ROM-log select) sampled at reset, and both are also wired to the SPI current sensor's CS# and DRDY#. Currently harmless (bring-up firmware only, and the daughter board idles them correctly at boot), but this needs re-verifying once the real driver is integrated into this firmware — confirm the daughter board never pulls either line to a level that would force an unintended boot mode during power-on.
